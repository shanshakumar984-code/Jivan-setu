01

OPD Consultation Shifts

Clinical slots are capped across fixed time windows to prevent doctor fatigue and overcrowding:

  • Morning OPD Shift: 09:00 AM – 01:00 PM (Routine check-ups & general visits)
  • Evening OPD Shift: 04:30 PM – 08:00 PM (Follow-up reviews & test evaluations)
  • Consultation Interval: 10 to 15 minutes allocated per patient
  • Emergency Buffer: 25% of hourly capacity reserved for acute arrivals
02

4-Stage Patient Queue Workflow

Patients move sequentially through standardized stages to maintain clinic records:

Stage 1
Token & Registration: Verified identity and slot matched at reception.
Stage 2
Triage & Vitals: Baseline BP, pulse, temperature, and SpO2 logged.
Stage 3
Physician Examination: Consultation in the doctor's cabin.
Stage 4
Digital Rx Generation: Prescription archived and printed.
03

Standard Clinical Vitals & Reference Ranges

Nursing triage records baseline parameters before the patient enters the consultation room:

Vital Parameter Standard Reference Range Critical Warning Flag Primary Clinical Objective
Blood Pressure (BP) 90/60 – 120/80 mmHg ≥ 140/90 or ≤ 85/55 mmHg Screen for hypertension, hypotension, or cardiac strain
Heart / Pulse Rate (HR) 60 – 100 beats per minute > 100 (Tachycardia) / < 55 bpm Detect arrhythmia, shock, fever response, or bradycardia
Body Temperature 97.8°F – 99.1°F (36.5°C – 37.3°C) ≥ 100.4°F (38.0°C) Identify active infection, pyrexia, or inflammatory conditions
Oxygen Saturation (SpO2) 95% – 100% (Ambient Air) < 93% (Hypoxemia) Evaluate respiratory airflow, asthma, or lung distress
Random Blood Glucose 70 – 140 mg/dL < 60 or > 200 mg/dL Rapid check for hypoglycemic collapse or diabetes
